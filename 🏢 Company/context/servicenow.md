# ServiceNow — Company Context

*Updated: April 2026. Source: FY2025 earnings, servicenow.com, public job postings.*

---

## What ServiceNow Is

ServiceNow is a **cloud-native enterprise SaaS platform** for workflow automation. They don't sell point solutions — they sell a horizontal platform (the **Now Platform**) that automates workflows across IT, HR, Customer Service, Finance, Legal, and Security. The strategy is land-and-expand: enter via IT (ITSM), then grow horizontally.

**Core belief**: Every department in every large enterprise has broken workflows. ServiceNow wins by being the single platform to fix them all.

## Business Model

- **Revenue**: 97%+ subscription; multi-tenant SaaS
- **Pricing**: Per-user/per-workflow licensing; AI features (Now Assist) moving toward consumption-based model
- **Services**: <10% of revenue; deliberately small — partners handle most PS
- **Expansion motion**: Land in IT, expand across the enterprise. Massive upsell opportunity in existing base.

## FY2025–2026 Strategic Priorities

1. **Agentic AI at enterprise scale** — "AI control tower for business reinvention." AI Agent Orchestrator + AI Agent Studio are the core platform bets.
2. **Now Assist growth** — moving to consumption pricing; net new ACV doubled in FY2025.
3. **CRM expansion** — directly targeting Salesforce's post-sales (account management, renewals, CS workflows).
4. **Platform consolidation** — one bank retired 256 applications onto Now Platform. Targeting 60-day enterprise implementations.
5. **Emerging products** — Workflow Data Fabric, RaptorDB Pro, CPQ, Moveworks ($2.85B acquisition) integration.
6. **Margin expansion** — 83.5% subscription gross margin (non-GAAP), targeting Rule of 50 leadership.

## Customer Excellence Group (CEG)

ServiceNow's **post-sales organization**. CEG owns:
- Go-to-Market
- Delivery / Implementation
- Customer Success
- Renewals
- Customer Health

Organized geographically: CEG Americas, CEG EMEA, CEG APAC, CEG Scale/Mid-Market.

There is a dedicated **CEG AI Center of Excellence (CoE)** producing agentic AI adoption playbooks for customers (and internally).

**CEG Business Intelligence Tooling** (led by Garin Landry) owns the internal data and tooling infrastructure that CEG runs on — dashboards, CS metrics, customer health scoring, renewal analytics, and the internal Customer Success Platform (CSP).

---

## "Now on Now" Program

ServiceNow uses its own platform to run its own business — this is a public program. The **"Now on Now CRM"** case study is public and shows:
- 40% customer churn risk mitigated via AI early detection of down-sell signals
- 27x faster analytics with RaptorDB

This is the organizational context for the CSP. The CSP is effectively ServiceNow's internal implementation of their own CSM/CRM product for the CEG.

## Competitive Positioning

ServiceNow positions against:
- **Salesforce** (CRM, post-sales workflows)
- **SAP / Oracle** (ERP process automation)
- **Gainsight / PlanHat** (Customer Success platform — via CSP/Now on Now CRM)
- **Point solutions** generally — the platform play is to retire them

Bill McDermott's framing: "CEOs are now demanding software losers" — meaning CEOs want to eliminate underperforming legacy software.
