# Customer Success Platform (CSP) — Product Context

*Updated: April 2026. Source: ServiceNow "Now on Now" public case study, job postings, GOALS.md.*

---

## What Is the CSP?

The **Customer Success Platform (CSP)** is an **internal ServiceNow product** — not a commercially sold product. It is:

- Built on the **Now Platform** (ServiceNow uses its own platform — "Now on Now" program)
- Used by **ServiceNow's Customer Excellence Group (CEG)** to manage post-sales operations
- The operational core for Customer Success Managers, Renewal Managers, and CS leadership managing ~8,800 customers globally

The CSP is ServiceNow's internal implementation of what the market would call a CS platform (like Gainsight or PlanHat). ServiceNow built their own rather than buying a point solution.

## Why This Product Matters

ServiceNow manages 85% of the Fortune 500. The CSP is how their 29K-employee post-sales organization tracks, manages, and grows that customer base. **Every point of NRR improvement across $13B ARR is significant.**

Additionally, the CSP is a **proof point for ServiceNow's own CRM/CS market expansion**. If Now Platform can run ServiceNow's own complex CS workflows, it validates the product for external enterprise customers considering Gainsight, PlanHat, or Salesforce alternatives.

## Competitive Context

The CSP implicitly competes with (or was built to replace):
- **PlanHat** — first competitive target named in project context
- **Gainsight** — dominant external CS platform
- **Salesforce Service Cloud / Success Cloud**

The strategic angle: by building on Now Platform, ServiceNow avoids paying a competitor and creates a reference architecture for their own CRM product expansion. The CSP is part of the CRM land-grab story.

## Strategic Framing

The CSP sits at the intersection of:
1. **Internal operational efficiency** — CEG can manage more customers with the same or fewer headcount
2. **Product validation** — proves Now Platform works as an enterprise CS platform
3. **Competitive displacement** — removes dependency on external CS vendors (PlanHat et al)
4. **AI surface area** — Now Assist + AI Agent capabilities can be applied to CS workflows

When pitching CSP work internally, frame it along these dimensions. It's not just internal tooling — it's a strategic asset.
