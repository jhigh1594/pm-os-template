# /deliver — Execution & Delivery

**Canonical skill:** `.claude/skills/execution-delivery/SKILL.md`

Operator-mode delivery support: quick status read on what's on track vs. at risk, priority focus for the current window, concrete next actions, and what to communicate to whom. For when work is in motion and you need a clear-headed read on it.

## Usage

`/deliver [current delivery situation or what's in flight]`

## Best Uses

- "Here's where my sprint stands — what's most at risk?"
- "We're behind on the DPD launch — what are the 3 moves that matter?"
- "I have three initiatives in flight and not enough bandwidth — triage this"
- "What do I communicate to stakeholders about the delay?"
- "Help me design a delivery cadence for the Q2 roadmap"

## Notes

- Default mode is triage (fast, operator-style) — use deep mode for quarter planning
- Distinct from `/launch` (go-to-market execution) — this is in-sprint/in-quarter delivery health
- Pairs with `/check-progress` and `/align`
