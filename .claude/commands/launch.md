# /launch — Launch Execution

**Canonical skill:** `.claude/skills/launch-execution/SKILL.md`

B2B-specific launch execution: tiers (limited / general availability), sales enablement assets, internal enablement, launch metrics, and go-live checklist. Distinct from strategy — this is the operational muscle for getting a shipped feature to market.

## Usage

`/launch [feature or product being launched]`

## Best Uses

- "Create a launch plan for the AgilePlace dependency intelligence feature"
- "What sales enablement do we need before GA?"
- "Build a launch tier framework for the DPD unified product"
- "What's the launch readiness checklist for OKR Key Results?"
- "Write the internal enablement brief for the sales team"

## Notes

- Use after `/spec` and PRD are complete — launch is post-build, pre-market
- Pairs with `/compete` (battlecard) and `/narrative` (pitch deck)
- Distinct from `/roadmap` (planning) — this is execution after the feature ships
