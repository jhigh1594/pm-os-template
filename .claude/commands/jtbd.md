# /jtbd — Jobs-to-be-Done

**Canonical skill:** `.claude/skills/jtbd-building/SKILL.md`

Applies Bob Moesta's Jobs-to-be-Done framework to shift from feature-speak to job-speak. Identifies what job a customer is trying to get done, what causes them to "hire" a product, and what progress they're trying to make.

## Usage

`/jtbd [feature, capability, or customer segment]`

## Best Uses

- "What job is AgilePlace being hired to do for Scrum Masters?"
- "Write job stories for the dependency visualization feature"
- "Why are customers firing [competitor] and hiring us?"
- "Map the JTBD for our DPD unified planning pitch"
- "What's the forcing function that causes teams to adopt OKRs?"

## Notes

- Use before positioning work — JTBD reveals the real job, not the stated one
- Pairs with `/discover` for customer interviews, `/spec` for requirements
- Outputs job stories: "When [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [outcome]"
