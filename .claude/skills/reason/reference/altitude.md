# Altitude Thinking Reference

The ability to zoom in and out is the rarest executive skill.

## The Three Levels

```
  30,000 ft  ═══ STRATEGIC ═══  Why? What does winning look like in 1-3 years?
  10,000 ft  ═══ OPERATIONAL ══ How do we organize to win? Quarterly plan?
   Ground    ═══ TACTICAL ═════ What do we do this week/sprint?
```

## Key Concepts

- **Altitude Mismatch**: when people are talking at different levels — the #1 cause of unproductive meetings
- **Bezos Type 1 vs Type 2 decisions**:
  - Type 1: Irreversible — need 90%+ confidence, move slowly
  - Type 2: Reversible — need 70% confidence, move fast, course-correct
- **Deloitte Zoom Out/Zoom In**: Skip the mushy 3-5 year middle. Zoom out to 10-20yr vision + zoom in to 6-12mo concrete actions
- **Disagree and Commit**: Debate at strategic altitude, commit fully at tactical altitude
- **Free Diving Rule**: Go deep when needed, always come back up for air (don't get lost in details)
