# Interview Prompts

Use these prompts to sharpen an ICP, validate a champion, and test the real alternatives.

Do not ask all of these in one call. Pick the smallest set that helps the next decision.

## Segment Fit

- Walk through the last time this problem showed up.
- What kind of company does this happen in most often?
- When does this problem become painful enough to act on?
- What tools or systems are usually already in place when this becomes a priority?
- What changed recently that made this worth looking at now?

## Champion Discovery

- Who usually notices this problem first?
- Who pulls together the first shortlist of options?
- Who has to live with the outcome if the decision goes badly?
- Who can push this forward internally without waiting for everyone to agree?
- Who is excited about solving this, and who is just reviewing risk?

## Alternatives

- If this product did not exist, what would the team do instead?
- What are they using today, even if it is messy?
- Have they tried to solve this internally?
- What other products typically make the shortlist?
- When buyers do nothing, what usually stops them?

## Urgency

- Why now instead of six months from now?
- What happens if this problem stays unsolved through the next planning cycle?
- What event tends to force action?
- Which metric, meeting, or executive pressure makes this feel urgent?

## Value Testing

- What outcome matters most to the person driving this decision?
- Which part of the current process is most frustrating?
- If this worked perfectly, what would get easier or better?
- Which result would make this feel worth the switch?
- Which claimed benefit sounds nice but would not actually change the buying decision?

## Objection Discovery

- What concerns come up after someone starts to like the idea?
- Who typically raises deployment or integration concerns?
- What makes the buying team hesitate, even when the value is clear?
- Which risks need proof before the deal can move forward?

## Qualification Follow-Ups

- How many teams are involved in this workflow today?
- How is the work tracked right now?
- How often does this problem show up?
- What is the current workaround costing in time, trust, or missed outcomes?
- Is there already budget or sponsorship for solving this?

## Red Flags

Treat these answers as caution signals:
- "Everyone is a buyer"
- "We are just exploring"
- "There is no real owner"
- "We do not feel pain today, but this could matter later"
- "We are interested, but nothing is changing this quarter"
