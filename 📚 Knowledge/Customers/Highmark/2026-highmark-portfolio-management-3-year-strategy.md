# Portfolio Management: Strategic Vision & 3-Year Goals

Our strategic vision outlines our commitment to elevating Portfolio Management Services over the next three years. By optimizing our resource allocation, enhancing data-driven decision-making, and streamlining operational processes, we aim to deliver greater efficiency, improved strategic alignment, and increased value across the organization.

---

## Drive Efficiency & Reduce Waste through Intelligent Capacity Management

Implement robust capacity and resource management capabilities to ensure optimal utilization and reduce operational waste.

- **Goal**: 15% improvement in resource allocation and utilization accuracy enables better project staffing decisions by Q3 2028
 - Establish capacity models
 - Enable data-driven allocation
 - Reduce project delays

---

## Elevate Strategic Choices with Integrated Architectural Insights

Improve visibility of business and technology impacts to support informed investment decisions.
- **Goal**: 80% integrated portfolio & architecture data (Lean IAs) by Q2 2027
 - **Goal**: 80% of projects/initiatives aligned with strategic objectives by Q1 2026
 - Unified impact framework
 - Enable scenario planning

---

## Automate Portfolio Operations with connected costing & planning

Reduce admin effort, improve accuracy, and strengthen costing & planning by connecting work to resources to finances.
- **Goal**: 75% of administrative/manual work reduction by Q1 2028
 - Implement automated timesheets
 - Improve experience & compliance

---

## Success Metrics & Key Performance Indicators

### Measurable Data Points
1. **Rationalize portfolio of SD tools** - Which directly relates to information consolidation and toolchain optimization. Evaluate:
   - Active projects managed in Planview vs. legacy tools
   - User login trends across legacy tools
   - License reduction or elimination

2. **Unified tool adoption** - Consolidated to <5 core tools for portfolio management, with >75% employee adoption. Success metrics:
   - Adoption rate by business unit
   - Feature utilization patterns
   - User satisfaction scores

3. **Strategic alignment achievement** - 80% of projects/initiatives directly aligned with strategic objectives. Measure via:
   - % of projects linked to strategic themes
   - Executive visibility into strategic progress
   - Stakeholder confidence in investment decisions

4. **Data quality & traceability score** - 20% improvement in average data quality score for key SDLC artifacts. Track:
   - Defect reduction rates
   - Traceability improvements
   - Testing efficiency gains

5. **Integrated data fabric** - Single source of truth: 80% integrated portfolio & architecture data (Lean IAs). Success metrics:
   - Data freshness
   - Cross-system consistency
   - Decision-making speed

6. **Efficient funding governance** - Streamlined intake, forecasting, and reporting. Measure:
   - Time to funding decisions
   - Accuracy of forecasts
   - Stakeholder satisfaction

7. **Funding model adoption** - Formalized funding model across business units. Monitor:
   - BUs using standardized model
   - Process compliance rates
   - Forecast accuracy

8. **Capacity planning integration** - 15% improvement in resource allocation and utilization accuracy. Success indicators:
   - Capacity forecast accuracy
   - Resource utilization rates
   - Project staffing efficiency

### Key Decisions & Assumptions
- **Rationalization of SD tools** - Reducing tool spraw by consolidating to Planview improves efficiency, reduces licensing costs, and creates a unified experience.
- **Unified tool adoption** - Consolidated tools (Planview, LeanIX, Timesheets) support standardized processes and data flow.
- **Strategic alignment achievement** - Aligning work with strategic objectives ensures resources are directed toward high-value initiatives.
- **Data quality & traceability score** - Addressing data quality issues in existing SDLC tools improves decision-making and reduces rework.
- **Integrated data fabric** - Integrating portfolio and architecture data enables comprehensive impact analysis and scenario planning.
- **Efficient funding governance** - Streamlined funding processes reduce administrative overhead and improve decision-making.
- **Funding model adoption** - A standardized funding model ensures consistency and scalability across the organization.
- **Capacity planning integration** - Connecting work to resources enables optimized capacity management and scenario planning.

### Process Risks & Mitigation
| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|------------|
| **Resistance to change** - Teams may resist adopting new tools and processes. | High | High | Change management program with clear communication, training, and support. |
| **Data quality issues** - Existing data in legacy tools may have quality problems. | Medium | High | Data cleansing and migration strategy, with clear data standards and validation. |
| **Integration complexity** - Connecting multiple systems (Planview, LeanIX, Timesheets) may be technically challenging. | Medium | Medium | Phased integration approach with clear milestones and dedicated technical resources. |
| **Adoption barriers** - Employees may struggle with new features and workflows. | Medium | Medium | Comprehensive training program and user-friendly documentation. |
| **Resource constraints** - Technical and PM resources may be stretched thin. | Medium | Medium | Prioritize high-value initiatives and leverage external expertise if needed. |
