# Decision Journal

A rolling log of significant decisions. Each entry is appended by `/decide`.
Reviewed weekly via `/weekly-review` (decisions section).

**How to use:**
- `/decide` appends a new entry at the bottom of this file
- `/weekly-review` surfaces entries where review-date ≤ today for outcome validation
- Entries are never edited — only the Outcome and What we learned fields are filled in at review time
- After 5+ entries, `/weekly-review` surfaces calibration patterns ("are we consistently over/under-confident?")

---

## Entry Template

```
---
**Decision:** [One sentence — what was decided]
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Type:** Type 1 (one-way door) | Type 2 (two-way door)
**Reversibility cost:** High | Medium | Low — [one sentence why]
**Confidence at decision time:** 70% | 80% | 90%+
**Options considered:** [A brief label for each option considered]
**Chosen:** [Option name]
**Rationale (30 words max):** [Core reason, key tradeoff made]
**Riskiest assumption:** [What must be true for this to work]
**Success criteria:** [How we'll know we were right — specific threshold, not direction]
**Review date:** YYYY-MM-DD  ← set at 30 days (Type 2) or 90 days (Type 1)
**Outcome:** [Filled at review: Met | Missed | Exceeded | Reversed — one sentence]
**What we learned:** [Filled at review — one to three sentences]
---
```
