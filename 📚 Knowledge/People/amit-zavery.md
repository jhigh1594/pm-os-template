## Background

- **25 years at Oracle**: Started as a software engineer; became Executive VP of Product Development overseeing cloud platform, middleware, security, analytics, and Java — a team of 4,500+ generating $6B+ annual revenue.
- **~6 years at Google Cloud**: VP/GM and Head of Platform. Helped scale Google Cloud from ~$7B to $41B+ annualized revenue, making it the 4th-largest enterprise software company.
- **Board member**: Broadridge Financial Solutions (NYSE: BR).
- Comes in as a platform-scale builder — the person who turns product vision into infrastructure reality.

## Why ServiceNow Brought Him In

McDermott explicitly sought someone with both deep technical platform expertise and proven enterprise scaling experience. The announcement: *"a proven history of building market-defining products and scaling world-class platforms."*

Zavery arrived during ServiceNow's highest-stakes product expansion — agentic AI, CRM entry, and platform consolidation simultaneously. His scope (product + engineering + cloud infra + UX + operations) signals a deliberate consolidation of execution authority at the top.

---

## Product Philosophy

**Outcome-first**: SaaS customers "don't care how you build GenAI — they care whether it works for their business." No technology for technology's sake.

**Platform thinking**: Oracle and Google Cloud shaped a deep belief in infrastructure as a competitive moat. The platform layer wins long-term; features are temporary.

**Scale discipline**: Built teams of thousands and $6B+ product lines. Brings enterprise engineering rigor, not startup energy.

## How to Align to Zavery's Direction

- **Platform framing first**: How does this work leverage or extend the Now Platform? Does it strengthen the platform moat?
- **Business outcome metrics**: Revenue impact, retention, efficiency. Not feature counts.
- **Technical credibility**: He's a deep technical leader — avoid hand-waving on architecture.
- **Scalability story**: Can this work for 10 customers? 1,000? The Now on Now CSP is a proof point before broader CEG rollout.
- **Agentic AI angle**: If your product touches AI, connect it to the Agent Orchestrator / AI Agent Studio roadmap.
