## Background

- Grew up working-class on Long Island; bought a delicatessen at age 16 before school — an "underdog origin" he references publicly and often.
- Career path: Xerox (youngest corporate officer at 36) → Gartner → Siebel Systems → SAP (CEO 2010–2019, grew market cap ~4x) → ServiceNow.
- Suffered a near-fatal accident that cost him one eye; continued leading through recovery. References this as deepening his drive and perspective.
- Under his tenure, ServiceNow crossed $13B annual revenue; positioned as an AI-native enterprise platform.

---

## Management Philosophy

**Underdog mindset**: Deliberately frames ServiceNow as the challenger, never the incumbent — even at $13B revenue. Walks into every room looking for the fight.

**Over-communication**: Core belief — *"Anything worth communicating is almost always undercommunicated."* Repeats strategic direction relentlessly until it sticks.

**People over automation**: Does not use AI to cut headcount. Publicly committed to upskilling all ~29K employees rather than downsizing. "There is no artificial intelligence without human intelligence."

**Transparency norms**: Expects problems surfaced with proposed solutions, never problems alone.

**AI posture**: All-in on agentic AI — not as a feature, but as the core platform direction. Describes ServiceNow as "the AI control tower for business reinvention."

---

## Communication Style

- High-energy, theatrical. Annual all-hands branded "ServiceNow Live" include motivational rallies and entertainment.
- Publicly evangelical — heavy media presence on CNBC, enterprise tech outlets.
- Loves competitive narratives; frequently names Salesforce, SAP, Oracle as targets.
- Repeats key phrases at scale: *"Playing chess, not checkers,"* *"It'll never be this slow again,"* *"CEOs are now demanding software losers."*

---

## Current Strategic Priorities (2025–2026)

1. **AI control tower** — orchestrate agentic AI across the enterprise; not just GenAI features, but autonomous agents.
2. **CRM expansion** — challenge Salesforce in post-sales and account management.
3. **Platform consolidation** — displace point solutions; land fast (targeting 60-day implementations).
4. **AI partnerships** — Anthropic (Claude) and OpenAI both named as partners.
5. **Workforce upskilling** — internal AI adoption at scale, not headcount reduction.

---

## How to Communicate with / Align to McDermott's Direction

If your work surfaces to Bill or needs executive sponsorship at his level:

- **Lead with the underdog angle**: How does this make ServiceNow more competitive?
- **Frame in outcomes**: Revenue impact, customer retention, market displacement.
- **Use his vocabulary**: "reinvention," "AI control tower," "platform consolidation," "chess not checkers."
- **Show speed**: Fast timelines and clear milestones signal alignment with his pace obsession.
- **Pair problems with solutions**: Never surface a problem without a recommended path forward.
