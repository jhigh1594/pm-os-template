## Daily CI Brief — AI-Native CS Platforms

These are the only competitors included in the automated daily `/ci-brief` run.
All are direct competitors to ServiceNow CSP in the customer success platform space.

| Company | Stage | Why it matters | Key monitoring focus |
|---------|-------|---------------|---------------------|
| Planhat | Private | **Primary target.** Purpose-built CS platform, strong mid-market traction. Most often compared to SNOW CSP in deals. | Pricing page, G2 reviews, new features, funding signals |
| Vitally | Private | Fast-growing CS platform, strong product velocity, beloved by CS ops teams. Positioned squarely in SNOW's ICP. | Product changelog, G2 reviews, job postings |
| Cust | Private | AI-agent-first CS platform; explicit positioning around AI agents replacing 1:many CS work. Direct threat to SNOW CSP's AI narrative. | Product releases, positioning language, G2 reviews |
| Velaris | Private | Series A funded, AI-native health scoring and proactive alerting. Growing fast in mid-market. | Funding news, feature launches, customer wins |
| EverAfter | Private | Enterprise digital CS / customer hub platform. Shortlisted alongside Planhat in enterprise deals. AI-personalized customer portals at scale. | Product updates, G2 reviews, partnership announcements |

**Key search targets per competitor:**
- `[competitor].com/pricing` — pricing tiers and feature gates
- `g2.com/products/[slug]/reviews` — live customer sentiment
- `[competitor].com/blog` or `changelog.[competitor].com` — product velocity signals
- LinkedIn/Greenhouse job listings — roadmap and growth signals
