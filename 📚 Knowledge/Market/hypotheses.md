## Active Hypotheses

### CS Investment Paradox — Coordination Model Problem
**Belief**: NRR decline despite increased CS spend is a coordination model failure, not a tooling gap. Buying better point tools won't fix it.
**Signal**: Bain 2024 — 75% of firms saw NRR decline; TSIA data on fragmented post-sales tooling; inferred from absence of cross-functional orchestration in all current CS platforms.
**Confidence**: Medium
**Test**: Customer interviews — do VP CS buyers describe their problem as "better CS tool" or "better cross-functional coordination"? Win/loss data on why ServiceNow loses to Gainsight.
**Confirmations**: 1 / 3
**Status**: active

### Cross-Functional Orchestration Whitespace
**Belief**: No existing CS platform owns the full Sales→CS→Support→PS→Product post-sales motion. This is the real whitespace — not another CS tool, but a workflow orchestration layer.
**Signal**: Competitive analysis of Gainsight, Planhat, Totango, Vitally — all are point solutions within CS; none coordinate across departments.
**Confidence**: Medium
**Test**: Test with 5 VP CS interviews — does the "cross-functional handoff breakdown" problem rank in their top 3 pain points unprompted?
**Confirmations**: 1 / 3
**Status**: active

### AI Adoption Blocked by Data Quality, Not Ambition
**Belief**: CS teams aren't failing to adopt AI because they don't want it — they can't because their data is fragmented across CRM, CS platform, product analytics, and support ticketing. Workflow-byproduct data unification is the real unlock.
**Signal**: ChurnZero 2025 — 72% say AI critical, 32% have live use cases; G2/TrustRadius patterns showing "integration" as top complaint across all CS platforms.
**Confidence**: Medium
**Test**: In customer interviews, ask "what would need to be true for you to trust an AI health score?" — does data quality surface unprompted?
**Confirmations**: 1 / 3
**Status**: active
